<h2>Value-based decision-making in social contexts</h2>
<p>We use economic probes of behavior together with functional neuroimaging and computational models to characterize how the brain processes social and non-social information.</p>

<h2>Computational psychiatry</h2>
<p>We leverage quantitative decision models to identify computational mechanisms underlying atypical decision-making and psychiatric symptoms.</p>
