Starting this September, I will be joining the Department of Brain Cognitive Sciences and SNU College at Seoul National University as an Associate Professor.

After many rewarding years at UNIST, I am excited to begin this new chapter at SNU. I am deeply grateful to UNIST for the support, opportunities, and wonderful colleagues who have made my time here such a meaningful part of my academic journey. I am especially thankful to the students, colleagues, and collaborators who have shared in the growth of our research and lab.

While it is bittersweet to leave UNIST, I look forward to new opportunities to expand our research and collaborations at SNU.
