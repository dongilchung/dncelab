<h1>Open Positions</h1>

<p>Currently, we are recruiting <a href="#graduate">graduate students</a> and <a href="#undergraduate">undergraduate research interns</a>.</p>
<p>Please see below for details.</p>

<h2 id="graduate">Graduate Students</h2>
<p>The <strong>Decision Neuroscience &amp; Cognitive Engineering Lab</strong> (PI: Dongil Chung; <a href="https://dongilchung.github.io/dncelab">dongilchung.github.io/dncelab</a>) is recruiting graduate students in decision neuroscience and computational psychiatry with an emphasis on functional neuroimaging and computational modeling. Ideal candidates will be interested in studying human value-based and/or social decision-making using neuroimaging, computational modeling, Bayesian modeling, and/or machine learning.</p>

<p>A successful candidate will have the opportunity to work on several projects. Specific research topics and training opportunities can be individually tailored. The lab will offer internationally competitive training and research opportunities.</p>

<p>A preference will be given to candidates who have:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Majored in cognitive or computational neuroscience, engineering, psychology, experimental economics, or a related field</li>
  <li>A strong interest in research about cognitive and decision neuroscience</li>
  <li>Excellent organizational, interpersonal, and communication skills</li>
  <li>Strong programming background (e.g., MATLAB, R, python)</li>
  <li>Professional work ethics (e.g., diligence, honesty)</li>
</ul>

<p>Preferred, but not necessary:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Previous research internship experiences</li>
  <li>Facility with signal processing or computational/quantitative methods</li>
</ul>

<p>How to apply:</p>
<p>To apply, please forward a statement of interest. Please make sure to address your experience and skill set. Consideration of applications will begin immediately and will end when the position is filled. Send inquiries and applications to Dr. Dongil Chung (<a href="mailto:dongilchung@snu.ac.kr">dongilchung@snu.ac.kr</a>).</p>

<h2 id="undergraduate">Undergraduate Research Intern</h2>
<p>The successful candidate will participate in all aspects of research in the lab, including: acquisition of behavioral and neural data, preparing consent forms and other research ethics related forms, assembling and administering neuropsychological assessments, screening and recruitment of participants, obtaining informed consent, scheduling participants for testing, tracking progress of the study and data collection, verifying data and entering pertinent information into database for statistical analysis, conducting statistical analysis, and assisting with manuscript preparation.</p>

<p>A preference will be given to candidates who have:</p>
<ul>
  <li>A strong interest in learning about cognitive and decision neuroscience</li>
  <li>Excellent organizational, interpersonal, and communication skills</li>
  <li>Some programming background (e.g., MATLAB, R, python)</li>
</ul>

<p>Preferred, but not necessary:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Prior experience running studies with human subjects is a plus</li>
  <li>Prior experience with Amazon Mechanical Turk, fMRI, EEG, psychophysiology, signal processing, statistical analysis, and neuropsychological assessments would all be an added advantage</li>
</ul>

<p>Please forward a statement of interest. Please make sure to address your experience and skill set. Consideration of applications will begin immediately. Send inquiries and applications to: Dr. Dongil Chung (<a href="mailto:dongilchung@snu.ac.kr">dongilchung@snu.ac.kr</a>)</p>
